# RWI – Runway Intelligence

## Vision

Build the world's most trusted intelligence platform for runway safety.

RWI transforms airport documents, infrastructure plans and public information into actionable intelligence.

Our goal is to help users understand what is happening, why it matters and what is likely to happen next.

---

## Mission

Collect.

Verify.

Understand.

Explain.

---

## What RWI will become

RWI will provide a complete view of airport runway safety worldwide.

Including:

- Airports
- Runways
- EMAS
- RESA
- Airport modernization
- Projects
- Documents
- Incidents
- Funding
- Procurement
- Contracts

---

## Core Principles

### Every fact has a source

### History matters
